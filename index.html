    How this tracker works
    • Overdue means the due date has passed (Riyadh date) and the action is not completed. "TBC" and blank due dates never count as overdue.
    • Data came from the Growth Office Excel tracker (Action Tracker sheet). Rows without a company label above them belong to the company label below them in the sheet.
    • Edit: click Edit (no password), then click any action to change it, or add new ones. Changes save instantly in your browser and are included in Export Excel straight away. Use "Share with team" to publish them so everyone sees them.
    • Export Excel gives a Summary sheet (totals update if you edit the Action Tracker sheet in Excel), the full Action Tracker with filters, and one sheet per company.
    • Import Excel (in edit mode): upload a newer version of the tracker, including one exported from here. Matching actions (same company and action title) are updated, new ones are added, and nothing is deleted.
    • The data is encrypted. Only people with the full private link can read it.
